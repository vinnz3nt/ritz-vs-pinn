A normed space is a vector space $V$ over a field $K$ equipped with a map (the norm) $\|\cdot\|: V \to \mathbb{R}$. 
For $v, u \in V$ and $\lambda \in K$ a norm satisfy the following:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Non-negativity: $\|u\| \geq 0, \quad \forall u \in V$.
    \item Zero vector: $\|u\| = 0 \Leftrightarrow u = 0$.
    \item Multiplicative distributivity: $\|\lambda u\| = |\lambda|\|u\|$.
    \item Triangle inequaltiy: $\|u+v\| \leq \|u\|+\|v\|$.
\end{enumerate}
